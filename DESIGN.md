# Design system

## Direction

Operate mode. A familiar, polished collaboration dashboard that combines the scannable navigation and presence of a community app with the straightforward room controls of a video meeting tool. Keep patterns recognizable; prioritize room discovery and getting into a live conversation.

## Palette and material

- Warm off-white page background with white content surfaces.
- Deep indigo/navy for primary emphasis and the active room.
- Clear green for connected/live state, with red reserved for destructive or leave actions.
- Fine neutral borders, restrained soft shadows, and 12–16px surface corners.

## Typography

Use a legible UI sans-serif with a clear weight and size hierarchy. Reserve monospace for room IDs and technical connection details.

## Composition

Desktop app shell with a compact navigation rail, a flexible central workspace, and a contextual right rail. On smaller screens, collapse these rails into stacked sections and keep primary actions reachable.

## Interaction

Native buttons and labeled form controls, visible keyboard focus, immediate feedback on media permission and connection state, and clear disabled/loading states.

## Product constraints

Call and livestream creation are equally prominent. Do not imply large-audience livestream scaling: the initial peer-to-peer implementation is for small rooms.
